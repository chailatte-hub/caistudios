Before we arrive

House calls only. A little preparation makes the reading more accurate.

  1. i.
    Good light

    A room with big windows is ideal. Otherwise, bright neutral lighting works.

  2. ii.
    A table and chairs

    Somewhere to lay out the drapes, and a seat for everyone in the session.

  3. iii.
    A large mirror

    One that shows you from the waist up. If you don't have one, we'll bring it.

  4. iv.
    A bare face

    Come with little to no makeup, and keep your makeup pouch handy.
